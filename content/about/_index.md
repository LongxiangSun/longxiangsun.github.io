# About me

Hello! I’m Longxiang Sun, or simply Longxiang. I’m a PhD candidate at the School of Geodesy and Geomatics, Wuhan University, where I study how the ground moves—which sounds simple until you actually try to measure a few millimeters of movement from space.

I’m still learning, and there is certainly much more that I don’t know than I do. But that’s also what makes research interesting. My goal is to keep improving how we observe and understand the deformation of Earth’s crust, one dataset, one model, and occasionally one stubborn piece of code at a time.
