# About Longxiang

Hello! I am Longxiang Sun–you can call me “Longxiang” for short (as all my friends do!), a PhD candidate at the School of Geodesy and Geomatics at Wuhan University. I study how the ground moves, which sounds simple until you actually try to measure a few millimeters of movement from space.

I am still learning, and there is certainly a lot more that I do not know than I do. But that is also what makes research interesting. My goal is to keep improving our ability to observe and understand how the Earth's crust deforms, one dataset, model, and occasionally stubborn piece of code at a time.
